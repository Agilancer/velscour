========== FIRST MESSAGE (attach the zip) ==========

You're the pixel artist for VELSCOUR, an isometric dark-fantasy action RPG in the style of Diablo II, drawn as
hand-placed 2D pixel art. The attached zip is a sprite kit for one asset: the Death Knight's Runeblade, a spectral
greatsword that hovers at his shoulder, takes a glowing rune in each of six slots as he fights, and then swells to
giant size for one colossal cleave.

Before drawing anything, open the zip and read every file:
- SPEC.md: exactly what to draw, the cell sizes, the anchor point, the rune slot positions and how the game uses each
  sprite. It wins over everything else.
- STYLE.md: the style lock (the pixel-art rules every VELSCOUR hero is drawn by) and the exact palette.
- templates/: the two sheets you'll fill in. Cyan dashed boxes are the cells; yellow lines are the centre line, the
  crossguard line (the anchor), the tip line and the pommel top; green marks are the blade width, the guard ends and
  the six rune slots.
- examples/: the same sheets filled with a flat mock-up. Copy the placement from them, not the look.
- reference/: the Death Knight at the sword's scale, the palette swatches, and the Runeblade as the game draws it today.

Then reply with:
1. The design card from STYLE.md (four lines), filled in for this sword.
2. A one-paragraph description of the sword: the blade, the guard, the grip, the pommel, the verdigris, and the three
   rune glyphs.
3. Anything in the spec that's unclear to you.

Don't make any images in this reply. I'll ask for one sheet per message.

========== SHEET 1 (attach templates/runeblade_sheet1_hover_template.png and examples/runeblade_sheet1_layout_example.png) ==========

Draw sheet 1, the hovering Runeblade and the small runes, as one 1536 x 1024 image. Use the attached template as the
exact layout: same canvas size, same five cell positions, same flat magenta #FF00FF background.

- Hard-edged pixel art on a grid where one art pixel is a 3 x 3 block of identical pixels. No blur, no gradients, no
  anti-aliasing against the magenta.
- HOVER: the sword pointing straight down on the yellow centre line, the centre of the crossguard on the yellow
  crossguard line, the point on the tip line, the pommel's top on the dotted line. A broad straight frost-glass blade
  12 art pixels wide with a long point and a fuller, a flat tomb-lid crossguard 34 art pixels wide with hourglass ends
  in verdigris bronze, a dark wrapped grip, an hourglass pommel with a small gem, verdigris tears under the guard.
  Six EMPTY rune slots engraved in the fuller exactly on the green squares. Use the hex colours from STYLE.md, with a
  1 art-pixel moonlit rim on the right edges.
- HOVER CHARGED: exactly the same sword, pixel for pixel, with the blade's edges and fuller kindled with the frost rune
  ramp and the pommel gem white-hot. The slots stay empty.
- RUNE S FROST, UNHOLY, BLOOD: one glowing glyph per cell, centred on the crosshair, about 5 x 7 art pixels, each in its
  own emissive ramp with a 1 art-pixel dark rim. Abstract marks, not letters: a rime star, a plague fork, a cut drop.
- No text anywhere. No halo painted outside any shape. Remove every label, box and guide line. Only the art on flat
  magenta.
