========== FIRST MESSAGE (attach the zip) ==========

You're the pixel artist for VELSCOUR, an isometric dark-fantasy action RPG in the style of Diablo II, drawn as
hand-placed 2D pixel art. The attached zip is a sprite kit for one asset: the Lancer's war banner, a banner on a pole
strapped to his back that unrolls as he fights and blazes when his knights charge.

Before drawing anything, open the zip and read every file:
- SPEC.md: exactly what to draw, the frame sizes, the anchor points and how the game animates it. It wins over
  everything else.
- STYLE.md: the pixel-art rules and the exact palette.
- templates/: the two sheets you'll fill in. Cyan dashed boxes are the frames, yellow lines are the centre line and
  the crossbar line, green lines are the cloth's full size.
- examples/: the same sheets filled with a flat mock-up. Copy the placement and the motion from them, not the look.
- reference/: the Lancer at the banner's scale, the palette swatches, and the banner as the game draws it today.

Then reply with:
1. The design card from STYLE.md (four lines), filled in for this banner.
2. A one-paragraph description of the cloth: its folds, the emblem, the wear, the swallowtail.
3. Anything in the spec that's unclear to you.

Don't make any images in this reply. I'll ask for one sheet per message.

========== SHEET 1 (attach templates/banner_sheet1_cloth_template.png and examples/banner_sheet1_layout_example.png) ==========

Draw sheet 1, the banner cloth, as one 1536 x 1024 image. Use the attached template as the exact layout: same canvas
size, same twelve frame positions, same flat magenta #FF00FF background.

- Draw only the cloth: no pole, no crossbar, no finial.
- Hard-edged pixel art on a grid where one art pixel is a 3 x 3 block of identical pixels. No blur, no gradients, no
  anti-aliasing against the magenta.
- The cloth hangs from the yellow crossbar line, centred on the yellow centre line, 40 art pixels wide and 88 long
  (the green box), with a hem sleeve and rings along the top and a swallowtail at the bottom.
- Night indigo field, a reliquary-gold border, and a centred emblem: a grave-steel tower shield with a portcullis grid
  and a gold lance across it. Use the hex colours from STYLE.md. A 1 art-pixel cold moonlit rim on the right edges.
  No text anywhere.
- Row 1, CALM 1-6: a gentle ripple travelling down the cloth, as one seamless loop (frame 6 flows into frame 1).
- Row 2, RALLIED 1-6: the same cloth in a strong gale: deeper folds, the tails snapping, a corner flicking up on
  frames 3-4. Also a seamless loop.
- The top edge never moves. Every frame shows the same cloth; only the folds and the sway change.
- Remove every label, box and guide line from your image. Only the art on flat magenta.

========== SHEET 2 (attach templates/banner_sheet2_parts_template.png, examples/banner_sheet2_layout_example.png and your sheet 1) ==========

Draw sheet 2, the banner parts, as one 1536 x 1024 image. Use the attached template as the exact layout, with the same

- POLE: a straight ash-wood pole up the centre line from the bottom yellow line to just above the crossbar, 3 art

- Everything centred on its frame's centre line. Remove every label, box and guide line. Only the art on flat magenta.
