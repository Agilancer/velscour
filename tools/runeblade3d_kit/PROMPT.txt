========== FIRST MESSAGE (attach the zip) ==========

You're the pixel artist for VELSCOUR, an isometric dark-fantasy action RPG in the style of Diablo II, drawn as
hand-placed pixel art. The attached zip is a texture kit for one asset: the Death Knight's Runeblade.

The Runeblade is a spectral greatsword that hovers at the Death Knight's side. It takes a glowing rune in each of six
slots as he fights, then grows to giant size for one colossal cleave. In the game it is a real 3D model. The game
builds that model from ONE picture you paint, which shows three views of the same sword pointing down:
- FRONT (face-on). This gives the outline and the front colours.
- SIDE (edge-on). This gives how thick the sword is at every height.
- BACK (from behind, mirrored). This gives the back colours.

Before drawing anything, open the zip and read every file:
- README.md: what the kit is.
- SPEC.md: the exact canvas, columns, guide lines and rune slot positions, and how the game turns the picture into 3D.
  It wins over everything else.
- STYLE.md: the style lock and the exact palette.
- templates/runeblade3d_texture_template.png: the sheet you'll fill in. The cyan dashed boxes are the three columns.
  The yellow lines are the centre lines, the pommel top, the crossguard centre and the point. The green squares are
  the six rune slots, and the green ticks mark the suggested blade and guard widths.
- examples/: the placeholder sword the game uses today, painted into the template. Copy the placement, not the look.
- reference/: how the sheet becomes 3D, the palette, and the placeholder model rendered in the game.

Then reply with:
1. The design card from STYLE.md (four lines), filled in for this sword.
2. One paragraph describing the sword: the blade, the guard, the grip, the pommel and the verdigris. Also say how
   thick each part is in the SIDE view.
3. Anything in the spec that's unclear to you.

Don't make any images in this reply. I'll ask for the sheet next.

========== THE SHEET (attach templates/runeblade3d_texture_template.png and examples/runeblade3d_layout_example.png) ==========

Draw the Runeblade texture sheet as one 1024 x 1536 portrait image. Use the attached template as the exact layout: the
same canvas size, the same three columns, the same guide lines, and a flat magenta #FF00FF background.

- Hard-edged pixel art on a grid where one art pixel is a 4 x 4 block of identical pixels. No blur, no gradients, and
  no anti-aliasing against the magenta.
- Draw the same sword three times, always pointing straight down on the column's yellow centre line. In every view
  the top of the pommel touches the top line, the middle of the crossguard is on the crossguard line, and the point
  touches the bottom line. Every part must be at the same height in all three views.
- FRONT: the sword face-on.
  - A broad, straight frost-glass blade about 144 px wide at the guard, with a long point and a darker fuller groove.
  - A flat tomb-lid crossguard about 360 px wide with hourglass ends in verdigris bronze. It is the widest part.
  - A dark wrapped grip, and an hourglass pommel with a small frost gem.
  - Verdigris tears running down from the guard.
  - Six EMPTY dark rune sockets in the fuller, exactly on the green squares.
- SIDE: the same sword turned 90 degrees, edge-on, at most 120 px wide:
  - the blade a thin wedge (about 32 px thick at the guard, thinning to the point);
  - the crossguard end-on (about 56 px deep);
  - the grip and pommel round, in profile.
  No rune sockets.
- BACK: the same sword seen from behind. Its outline is exactly FRONT's outline mirrored left to right. Use the same
  materials and the same six empty sockets on the green squares.
- Gentle, even light from the upper left. Shade with flat bands along the palette ramps in STYLE.md only. The game
  lights the model itself, so paint no hot highlights, deep shadows or cast shadows.
- No text anywhere, no lit runes, and no glow or halo outside the sword. Remove every label, box and guide line, so
  only the three views are left on flat magenta.

========== IF THE SHEET COMES BACK WRONG (use what applies) ==========

- "The pixels aren't even. Redraw it with every art pixel as a crisp 4 x 4 block of one colour."
- "The three views don't line up. Put the pommel top, crossguard centre and point of ALL THREE views on the same
  three yellow lines, so every part is at the same height in each."
- "BACK doesn't match FRONT. Make BACK's outline exactly FRONT's outline mirrored left to right."
- "SIDE is too wide. It's the sword edge-on: thin blade, crossguard seen end-on, at most 120 px wide."
- "The sword leans or sits off the centre line. Keep every view straight down its yellow centre line."
- "The rune sockets moved or are lit. Put six EMPTY dark sockets exactly on the green squares, in FRONT and BACK."
- "The lighting is too strong. Remove the bright streaks and dark shadow side; keep only flat material bands. The
  game lights the model in 3D."
- "There's a glow or halo outside the sword. Keep everything inside the outline on flat magenta."
- "There are labels or guides left in the image. Give me the same art on plain flat magenta only."
- "The colours drifted. Use only the hex values in STYLE.md."
- "It's the wrong size. The canvas must be 1024 x 1536, portrait."
