# The Veilscar March

Companion overworld biome for The Eternal Breach. The pack includes 60 floors, five modular wall families, three arches, three doors with open and closed states, and 40 scatter props (236 transparent frames total). See `../docs/The_Veilscar_March_Vision.md` for the area brief.

Open `START_HERE.html` for the offline browser, `manifest.json` for pivots and metadata, and `Overview.png` for the assembled preview. The floor basis is 160×80 pixels with 2:1 dimetric projection. Use binary-alpha PNGs and nearest-neighbor filtering in your engine.
