# Complete heads and feet — current user priority

The user explicitly reinforced that heads must never be cut off. Use a complete approved class head as the identity reference. Some older approved source images themselves touch the top edge; do not reproduce that cropping in new artwork.

Generate generous clear space around the full hair crown, both soles and all costume extensions. Inspect the whole generated sheet before extraction. Then isolate each complete figure rather than trusting nominal cell rectangles: a complete sleeve, hair lock or skirt can cross a nominal separator. Exclude neighboring figures and disconnected extraction fragments without repainting the artwork.

Check the saved individual PNG, not only the generator preview. Confirm that the entire head, hair, face, hands, feet and armor silhouette are present and clear the frame. If the original generated body meets the source-sheet edge, padding alone cannot prove missing anatomy was restored: visually inspect and use an image edit to repair actual clipping.

For this collection, the clipped Barbarian Epic and Legendary crowns were repaired through image editing; Uncommon received a framing repair too. Superseded files remain in work/, outside the delivered collection.

Source-art framing review does not certify exact canonical AP coordinates, head-pixel identity, uniform AP blocks or palette limits for game exports.
