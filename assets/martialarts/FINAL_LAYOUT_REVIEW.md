# Collection 05 — final visual review

144 individual sprites saved and reviewed: 48 Monk, 48 Rogue, 48 Ninja. Each class has eight Common, eight Uncommon, eight Rare, eight Epic, eight Legendary and eight Mythic designs.

Inspected each generated full source sheet before extraction, then each eight-outfit review board with isolated figures, original approved style reference and exact 60px-height versions. Reviewed identity/anatomy/stance, full head and soles, two empty hands, material definition, costume construction, repeating patterns and supporting attachments. Monk Uncommon 01 initially repeated the Common default too closely; replaced through a targeted image edit with a sleeveless padded double-cross vest, square tied sash, short thigh panels and russet trousers. Rechecked corrected source and review board. Rejected source/outfit are stored outside the delivered collection in work/collection05_superseded.

After assembly, visually inspected all six overview sections (24 figures each), all 144 exact60px display figures and the final collection preview. All figures are present in documented order, with clear complete heads/hoods, fists and boots. The Ninja retains full black head covering and narrow eye slit as specifically requested. Source colors and shading remain rich; no palette conversion was substituted for source quality.

Separately reopened all 21 assembled sheets from disk: 18 eight-outfit sheets at 1536x1024 and three 48-outfit class sheets at 1536x1536. Checked RGBA/transparency, dimensions, all 288 placements, cell clearance and presentation baseline. Individual files decode as RGBA with transparent borders. No extracted substantial body component touched a source-sheet edge before padding. Hashes are unique and do not match earlier delivered collections; this is a file-integrity check, not proof of visual novelty by itself.

Native source images are kept separately from resized presentation atlases. This review does NOT certify canonical AP landmarks, uniform integer AP blocks, exact head-pixel identity, game palette budgets, binary alpha or runtime integration. No game export/import was performed.
