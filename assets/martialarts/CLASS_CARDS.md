# Three new class identities — additions, not changes to existing classes

## Monk
Athletic muscular adult MAN, completely BALD smooth scalp, clean-shaven strong square jaw, medium warm olive-brown skin, brown eyes, calm stern expression. Not the pale gaunt Necromancer. Same head/age/build across 48 outfits.

Ascetic guardian of a ruined sun monastery. Muted yellow ochre and saffron garb, dark wooden prayer beads, russet and aged bronze secondary. Large regular prayer beads remain a signature in every tier. Powerful disciplined martial artist; original design, no copied Diablo character.

## Rogue
Athletic adult WOMAN, short BLACK pixie-cut hair just below the ears, defined cheekbones, warm light-medium skin, dark brown eyes, alert composed expression. Practical fully clothed female anatomy, no breast cups or cleavage. Same face and haircut across 48 outfits.

Black-leather infiltrator from the shuttered guilds of a gothic city. Charcoal and oiled black leather dominate, restrained oxblood or pewter accents. Knife-focused agility expressed through split hems, flexible gussets and reinforced forearms, not weapons.

## Ninja
Lean athletic adult masculine ninja, entire head/body covered in BLACK cloth, fitted black hood and lower face wrap, ONLY a narrow horizontal eye slit shows normal dark brown eyes and tiny surrounding skin. No visible hair, nose, mouth or ears. Same covered head and slit across 48 outfits.

Silent agent of a ruined moon temple. Full BLACK ninja suit with cloth wrapping, fitted flexible shadow armor, muted charcoal value separation, very restrained dark iron or midnight-indigo accents. Katana/shuriken combat role informs mobility only; no weapons in body sprites. Never turn into a knight, samurai or exposed-face rogue.

The first Common outfit is the default identity anchor. It is generated for this collection, not an original user-approved reference. Original approved references remain the style authority. User requested Ninja hood/eye slit overrides generic bare-head and faceless-default bans. Rogue black leather and Monk yellow garb are explicit current direction. Weapons describe combat roles only; hands remain empty for body armor.
