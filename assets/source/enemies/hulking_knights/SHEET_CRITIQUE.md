# VELSCOUR Hulking Castle Knights — sheet critique

Each final sheet was checked for usable silhouettes, intentional anatomy, in-cell weapon and limb containment, silhouette variety, and appropriate dungeon materials.

| Sheet | Review and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Lower Ward | Blood-red armor reads in silhouette, with long blades, a flail, and heavy shields. |
| Armoury and Forge | Grey-and-gold fittings distinguish the Forge knights. Gauntlets and the tower shield break up the polearms. |
| Ancient Library | Blue and silver read consistently. The hinged folio shields and longbow stay inside their cells. |
| Conservatory and Menagerie | Leaf-shaped weapons and greenhouse iron distinguish this wing. The net and cleaver stay readable. |
| Sanctuaries and Ossuary | Ivory plate and red cloth contrast clearly. Spear, arbalest and flail profiles stay separate. |
| Crimson Castle Bosses | The drawbridge shield, ram mace, and heavy arbalest give the Crimson bosses distinct profiles. |
| Forge Castle Bosses | Forge bosses use graphite plate and aged gold, with weapon variety and clear grips. |
| Library Castle Bosses | The codex shield, folding arbalest stand, and giant key give the Library bosses distinct equipment. |
| Conservatory Castle Bosses | Trellis, ivy and leaf motifs distinguish the armor. Crossbows and axes remain legible. |
| Ossuary Castle Bosses | The ivory plates stay readable against dark iron. The censer and oversized shields clear adjacent cells. |
