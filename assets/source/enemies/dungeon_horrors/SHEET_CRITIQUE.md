# VELSCOUR Dungeon Horrors — sheet critique

Each final sheet was checked for usable silhouettes, intentional anatomy, in-cell weapon and limb containment, silhouette variety, and appropriate dungeon materials.

| Sheet | Review and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Charnel Vaults | Pass. The ribs and wicker frames attach to readable shoulders and back. Porters and scribes have distinct stances, and the hooks clear the neighboring cells. |
| Flooded Crypts | Pass. The brine-dark cloth and oxidized fittings unify the sheet. Lung sacs, the grate, and handbells have clear points of support. |
| Gaols and Oubliettes | Pass. The racks, locks, and pillory collars read as attached equipment. Knuckle Man and the Tendon Cuff Watchman have visibly different arm proportions. |
| Sacristies | Pass. Candle wax, bell racks, and cage reliquaries create distinct profiles. The staffs and suspended censers remain inside their cells. |
| Abandoned Barracks | Pass. The arbalist braces his crossbow, the pavise follows the recruit's back, and the pike points clear of the neighboring silhouette. |
| Fungal Passages | Pass. Growths attach to shoulders and arms, with the mushroom cap, spore back, and puffball elbows providing distinct reads. The fencer's blade remains intact. |
| Alchemist Cellars | Pass. The retorts and jars are strapped to bodies or held. The Mortar Cranium and Filter Mask give clear contrasting profiles. |
| Glass and Mirror Halls | Pass. Mirrors read as backs, masks, or shields attached to a host. The Silver Facet's kneeling pose reads differently from the mirror carriers. |
| Collapsed Masonry | Regenerated. The original lintel and beam carriers repeated the same horizontal shape, and the pulley mechanism was unclear. The replacement uses a vertical stone frame, crossed timber rafters, and a yoke-mounted wheel with a visible rope grip. The replacement passed the cutout and silhouette checks. |
| Sealed Sanctuaries | Pass. The bellows, lantern, brazier chest, and harp each have a visible connection to the figure. Hooded silhouettes vary through hand and garment shapes. |
| Charnel and Drowned Bosses | Pass. The loom bracing, wheeled chair, and pallbearer's casket read as supported by their bodies. Each boss has a separate visual center, with room around the hooks and staffs. |
| Gaol and Barracks Bosses | Pass. The oversized hand joins a visible forearm, the shield is braced, and the trumpet points clear of adjacent bosses. Broad and narrow silhouettes alternate. |
| Fungal and Occult Bosses | Pass. The staircase and lintel have visible load-bearing support. The glass and mirror bosses distinguish faceted edges from rounded frames, and weapons stay inside their cells. |
