VELSCOUR — DUNGEON HORRORS

100 horrific humanoid monsters and 30 bosses. All are single-pose Grim Nocturne pixel art.

13 transparent 5 x 2 sheets plus 130 individual transparent PNGs. The individual PNGs can be placed in named monster-family source folders for the VELSCOUR enemy art pipeline. This archive supplies artwork and metadata. See docs/ENEMY_ART.md in VELSCOUR for adding bestiary entries and processing atlases.

Roster sheets and sprites use a top-left coordinate origin. Rectangles are x, y, width, height. Pivots are suggested sprite foot or support anchors, given in both the individual and sheet coordinate systems. Render at nearest-neighbor. Colors use Grim Nocturne ramps, capped at 24 colors including transparency for monsters and 32 for bosses. Standard monsters range from 104–121 AP tall. Bosses range from 228–288 AP.

SHEET_CRITIQUE.md records the visual review and any redraws.
