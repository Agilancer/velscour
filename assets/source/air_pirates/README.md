# Air Pirates — VELScour Character Pack

30 humanoid Air Pirate enemies and 10 humanoid Air Pirate bosses, designed as original opponents who board and attack other airships. Individual PNGs are transparent, hard-alpha, and palette-limited to the Grim Nocturne guidance.

Regular enemy sprites use the standard 128×192 AP frame and 100–104 AP figure scale. Bosses use a larger 256×320 frame and 180–212 AP figure scale, within the style bible's 162–288 AP boss range. All figures are original human pirates in three-quarter views with boarding weapons, rigging gear, naval coats, goggles, and restrained plum/gold accents.

Use nearest-neighbor filtering. `catalogue.json` and `catalogue.csv` contain names, roles, descriptions, dimensions, colors, and source cells. Labeled QA contact sheets are in `qa/`.
